*Atlas of the Heart* gave me a clearer vocabulary for emotions and helped me understand how shame, fear, and insecurity shape my relationships with other people and with my family. Brown's idea of “foreboding joy” stayed with me because I often meet good news with cynicism, waiting for something to go wrong. Social media can reinforce that posture without helping me understand its source. This book gives me a researched framework for noticing the difference between justified concern and reflexive pessimism. I return to it because naming these feelings makes it easier to acknowledge my fears and let some of them go.
