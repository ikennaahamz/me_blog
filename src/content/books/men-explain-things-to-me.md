I was floored by the precision of Rebecca Solnit's writing and by the way she draws larger arguments from deeply human stories. The scene in which a man confidently explains Solnit's own book to her is funny, but the confidence behind the mistake also reveals something more troubling. I read the collection in late 2025, years after it first appeared, and its ideas still felt painfully current. That freshness is unfortunate, but it is also why I expect to return to it. As long as the world is still learning how power shapes who gets heard, it will continue to need readers of this book.
