CRM for Blog Glossary

A small vocabulary for turning blog attention into durable reader relationships.

CRM

A system for remembering people, context, and next actions. For a blog, the "customer" may be a reader, subscriber, collaborator, recruiter, or future client.

Contact

One person in the system. Minimum useful fields: email, name if available, source, interest, consent status, and last meaningful interaction.

Source

Where the person came from, such as homepage, a specific essay, GitHub, LinkedIn, or a referral.

Tag

A flexible label for internal organization, such as interested-ai, project-uniblood, or potential-collaborator.

Segment

A saved group of contacts matching criteria. Example: readers tagged interested-ai who subscribed from the blog.

Lead magnet

A useful thing offered in exchange for an email address. For this blog, a good first version could be "monthly notes from what I learned and shipped."

Nurture

A deliberate follow-up path that builds trust. This can be as simple as one welcome email asking what topics the reader cares about.

Consent

The reader's permission and expectations around being contacted. Track it clearly; do not hide subscription intent inside vague contact forms.
