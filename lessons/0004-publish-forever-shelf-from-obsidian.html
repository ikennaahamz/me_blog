Lesson 0004

From your Obsidian hub to The Forever Shelf

Use Obsidian for honest drafting, Astro for validated publishing, and GitHub for deployment.

The core idea

Your Obsidian note is the working hub, not the public website. A book appears on the blog only after its finished entry is converted into the Astro book collection, passes validation, and is pushed to GitHub. This is a content publishing workflow, not a customer relationship management system.

The five-step publishing loop

  1. Draft: add a title, author, and rough personal reasons in the Forever Shelf Obsidian note.
  2. Declare readiness: finish the reason and remove the unfinished marker from that book.
  3. Edit: ask Codex to polish the entry without inventing opinions or changing your meaning.
  4. Publish: convert the ready entry into one Markdown file in the Astro books collection.
  5. Verify and push: run the production build, preview the page, commit, and push to GitHub.

What changes during publishing

Obsidian draft

Prompts, rough language, unfinished markers, and internal links are welcome here.

Astro entry

Validated frontmatter holds the title, author, surname sort key, cover details, ISBN, and draft state.

Published page

The polished 60–100-word reason becomes the entry body, sorted by author surname on the shelf.
